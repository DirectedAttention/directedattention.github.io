Ilya Sutskever describes SSI as a straight-shot research company

SSI · Interview / Public Signal · 2025-11-26

Back to Leaderboard Open source

Summary

In a long-form conversation with Dwarkesh Patel, Ilya Sutskever discussed SSI’s straight-shot approach to superintelligence, alignment, learning from deployment, self-play, multi-agent training, and the claim that AI is returning to an age of research.

Why it matters

This is one of the clearest public signals about SSI’s posture. SSI is not presenting itself as a product company with a near-term application layer. It is presenting itself as a research company aimed directly at safe superintelligence.

Leaderboard impact

This update strengthens SSI’s position as the clearest direct superintelligence project, while leaving the architectural-transparency question mostly open.
