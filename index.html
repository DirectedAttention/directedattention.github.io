Linguistic Agents advances a unique approach to AI called Symbolic Superintelligence.

We are building Symbolic Superintelligence.

The approach starts from the faculty of language. A system that aims at Superintelligence must account for the basic human capacity that connects intention, linguistic computation, and external signal.

From Alice to Bob

Linguistic Agents treats communication between agents as a long pipeline. Alice does not merely produce text, and Bob does not merely receive text. Each side uses a language-faculty architecture.
